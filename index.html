The Anatomy of a Classic

What is
Misal, really?

At its heart, Misal is a fiery sprout curry — usal — made from moth beans or mixed sprouts, slow-cooked in a deeply spiced gravy. It is crowned with crispy farsan, fresh onions, a squeeze of lemon, and a generous ladle of tarri — the floating orange-red spiced oil that defines its character. Served with soft pav, it is a complete universe in a single bowl.

Simple on the surface. Infinitely complex underneath. That is the beauty of Misal.

🌿
Matki Usal

The soul of Misal. Sprouted moth beans slow-cooked in a thick, aromatic base of onions, coconut, and Maharashtrian spices.

🌶
Tarri (Kat)

The fiery red-orange oil that floats on top. The more tarri, the more serious the Misal. This is where the heat lives.

✨
Farsan & Sev

The crispy, crunchy topping — chiwda, thin sev, and farsan — that gives Misal its legendary texture contrast.

🥥
Kanda-Tomato

Fresh chopped onions and tomatoes added raw on top, providing the essential coolness to counter the heat.

🍋
Lemon Squeeze

That final squeeze of lemon that brightens every flavour and ties the whole bowl together. Non-negotiable.

🍞
Ladi Pav

The soft, pillowy companion. Torn and dunked into the gravy, it is the perfect instrument to finish every last drop.

Across the State

The Regional Wars

Every region of Maharashtra will tell you theirs is the best. They are all right.

Pune
Puneri Misal
MEDIUM

The original, refined and balanced. A slightly sweet, mildly spiced usal with a thin tarri. Famous for the Chitale and Bedekar varieties. The standard by which all others are judged.

The Classic
Nashik
Nashik Misal
SPICY

Bolder and more robust than Pune. The usal is richer, the tarri is thicker, and the flavours are more intense. Nashik Misal is unapologetically itself — and proud of it.

Our Home
Kolhapur
Kolhapuri Misal
LEGENDARY

Not for the faint of heart. The Kolhapuri version uses the fiery Kolhapuri masala — a dark, deeply complex spice blend — making it the most intimidating and revered Misal in Maharashtra.

The Legend
Mumbai
Mumbai Misal
HOT

Street energy in a bowl. Mumbai Misal is chaotic and wonderful — loaded high with extra farsan, topped with everything, eaten on the go. It is the city in food form: fast, layered, unforgettable.

More than food

The Misal
Way of Life

Misal is deeply woven into the social fabric of Maharashtra. It is the dish that brings together the labourer and the businessman, the student and the professor — all standing at the same counter, eating from the same menu.

01
A Morning Tradition

Misal is quintessentially a breakfast dish. By noon at most legendary spots, the vessel is empty. You either show up early, or you wait till tomorrow.

02
The Debate Never Ends

Ask any Maharashtrian which Misal is best and you will start an argument that lasts for hours. The debate is part of the culture.

03
Generational Loyalty

Families have been eating at the same Misal stall for three generations. When a legendary spot closes, it makes the news.
